Galle private villa crafted for coastal getaways

Experience the comfort of a private Galle address with the ease of a resort-style stay.

The Dutch Inn - Galle is a four-bedroom private villa with three bathrooms, a living room, kitchenette, outdoor swimming pool, terrace, garden, breakfast, family rooms, free WiFi in public areas, free private parking, and room service. Set at No.130 D. J. Wimalasurendra Mawatha, the villa places guests within easy reach of beaches, Galle Fort, cafes, transport, and local culture.

Historic charm

Colonial character with a garden setting

This charming house carries Dutch colonial character through its red-terracotta tiled floor, whitewashed walls, wooden shutters, carved door, polished foyer floors, high ceilings, mouldings, antique furnishings, and vintage Dutch tiles.

Breakfast and activities

Crafted mornings and local culture experiences

Breakfast includes Full English or Irish and Asian selections with local specialities, warm dishes, juice, and fruits. Activities include a swimming pool, cooking class, tour or class about local culture, and a hands-on batik workshop by reservation. The batik workshop lasts 4-5 hours and is led by a local artisan.
