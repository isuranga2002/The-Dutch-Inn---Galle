Our story

An inn with a view of the world.

For three centuries Galle has been a meeting place — of merchants, monsoons, and slow southern evenings. The Dutch Inn is our chapter of that story: a heritage townhouse with louvered shutters and polished teak, a verandah heavy with antique cabinets, and a turquoise pool to disappear into when the day gets too hot.

We are family-run, intimate by design, and committed to the kind of stay that feels less like a hotel and more like staying with a well-travelled friend.

Small comforts,
carefully kept.

We don't promise marble lobbies. We promise hot ginger tea at 6am, a tuk-tuk waiting at dawn, and a host who knows where the best kottu is served — at the right price.

Free Wi-Fi & Workspace

Fibre-line internet across every room and the courtyard, with a quiet teak desk for those still-working-on-holiday mornings.

Turquoise Swimming Pool

An open-air pool set against the heritage facade — sun-warm by day, lantern-lit by night. Towels and king-coconuts always within reach.

Ceylon Breakfast

Hoppers with seeni sambol, fresh king-coconut, papaya and a pot of Ceylon tea — served until you've had enough.

Concierge by Heart

Tuk-tuks, whale-watching, train tickets to Mirissa, Sinharaja jeep tours — arranged, vetted, and at fair price.

Heritage Building

A century-old townhouse, lovingly restored. Original tile floors, teak doors, and the kind of shutters that make a verandah a verandah.

Airport Transfers

Pre-arranged car from Bandaranaike (CMB) or Mattala — fixed price, English-speaking driver, cold towels on arrival.

Free Cancellation

Most rates are fully refundable up to 48 hours before arrival. Travel plans change — we understand.

BBQ, Carrom & Shisha Nights

Pool-side BBQ evenings, a carrom board kept ready on the verandah, and a shisha lounge for the slow hours after dinner.
